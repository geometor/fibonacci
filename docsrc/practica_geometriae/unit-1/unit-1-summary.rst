UNIT I SUMMARY — PROLOGUE, PISAN METROLOGY & DISTINCTIO I–II
============================================================

.. rubric:: *Structural Synopsis of Boncompagni Vol. II, pp. 1–30 (fol. 1r–18v)*

:ref:`English Edition <unit-1-en>` · :ref:`Latin (1220 / Boncompagni 1862) <unit-1-la>` · :ref:`GEOMETOR Notes <unit-1-notes>`

----

Overview
--------

Composed in Pisa in $1220$ for Master :person:`Dominicus <dominicus>`, Unit I establishes the dual epistemological charter of *Practica Geometriae*: uniting rigorous Euclidean synthetic demonstration (*secundum geometricam demonstrationem*) with the concrete computational algorithms of Mediterranean surveyors (*secundum vulgarem consuetudinem*).

Key Structural Movements
------------------------

1. **Architectonic Plan of the Eight Distinctions** (:ref:`fol. 1r <unit-1-prol-en>`):
   :person:`Leonardo Pisano <fibonacci>` organizes the entire science of practical geometry into a progressive ladder:

   * *Dist. I–II*: Rectangular mensuration, Euclidean geometric algebra, and square roots;
   * *Dist. III–IV*: General planar mensuration (triangles, polygons, circles) and consortium land division;
   * *Dist. V–VI*: Cube roots, solid stereometry, and the five regular polyhedra in a sphere;
   * *Dist. VII–VIII*: Optical surveying with the :term:`quadrans` and higher algebraic-geometric *subtilitates*.

2. **Euclidean Foundations** (:ref:`fol. 1v–2v <unit-1-intro-en>`):
   Definitions of point, straight line, plane surface, right angle / :term:`cathetus`, and solid body from :cite:`euclid:elements` Books I and XI, paired with Common Notions $1$–$5$.

3. **The Pisan Agrarian Lattice** (:ref:`fol. 2v–3v <unit-1-pertica-en>`):
   Linear and superficial conversion hierarchy anchored on the $6$-foot Pisan :term:`pertica`:

   .. list-table:: Pisan Superficial & Linear Hierarchy
      :header-rows: 1
      :widths: 26 37 37

      * - Unit
        - Definition
        - Exact Equivalent in *Perticae*
      * - **Linear Pertica**
        - $6$ linear :term:`pedes <pes>` $= 108$ linear :term:`unciae <uncia>`
        - $1$ linear *pertica*
      * - **Superficial Pertica**
        - $1\text{ pertica} \times 1\text{ pertica}$ ($6 \times 6\text{ pedes}^2$)
        - $1$ ($= 36$ *denarii* $= 108$ sup. *unciae*)
      * - **Superficial Pes**
        - Strip $1\text{ pertica} \times 1\text{ linear pes}$
        - $\frac{1}{6}$ *pertica* ($= 6$ *denarii*)
      * - **Superficial Denarius**
        - Unit square $1\text{ linear pes} \times 1\text{ linear pes}$
        - $\frac{1}{36}$ *pertica*
      * - **Panorum**
        - Seed-basket measure of land
        - $5\frac{1}{2} = \frac{11}{2}$ *perticae* ($198$ *denarii*)
      * - **Stariorum**
        - $12$ *panora* ($12 \times \frac{11}{2}$)
        - $66$ *perticae* ($2{,}376$ *denarii*)

4. **Distinctio I — Rectangular Mensuration & Euclid Books II–III** (:ref:`fol. 4r–11v <unit-1-dist1-en>`):
   Area (:term:`embadum`) of squares and rectangles, cross-multiplication table for mixed subunits, the $1$-*stariorum* ($66$ *perticae*) frontage division ($66 \div 7 = 9\text{p } 2\text{ft } 10\frac{2}{7}\text{in}$), Euclid II.1–10 identities ($(a+b)^2$, $ad \cdot db + gd^2 = gb^2$), and intersecting chords (Euclid III.35).

5. **Distinctio II — Positional & Fractional Square Roots** (:ref:`fol. 11v–18v <unit-1-dist2-en>`):
   Hindu-Arabic digit-pair extraction, two-step rational Heron/Babylonian refinement $a_2 = a_1 - \frac{a_1^2 - N}{2a_1}$, and reduction of fractional areas ($\sqrt{\frac{2}{3}\text{ pertica}} = \sqrt{24\text{ denarii}} \approx 4\text{ pedes } 16\frac{2}{11}\text{ unciae}$).
