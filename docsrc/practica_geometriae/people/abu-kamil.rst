Abū Kāmil Shujāʿ ibn Aslam
==========================

**Role**: *Egyptian Algebraist ('The Reckoner')*

Author of *On the Pentagon and Decagon* and the *Book of Algebra*, whose biquadratic equations (*census census*) for inscribing regular polygons inside a circle of diameter :math:`10` directly inspired *Distinctio VIII*.
