Distinctio V — Incipit Quinta de Radicibus Cubicis Inveniendis
--------------------------------------------------------------

*Versio Anglica*: :ref:`Distinctio V — Cube Roots (pp. 148–159) <unit-4-dist5-en>` · *Commentarius*: :ref:`Notae ad Radices Cubicas <unit-4-s1-notes>`

.. enunciation::

   Explicit distinctio quarta; incipit quinta de radicibus cubicis inveniendis.

Quemadmodum in secunda distinctione ex compositione quadrati ostensa est inventio radicum quadratorum, ita ex sectione cubi $(a + b)^3 = a^3 + 3a^2 b + 3ab^2 + b^3$ ostenditur inventio radicis cubice per ternarios figurarum, cum regula propinquationis in numeris non cubicis $a^3 + R$:

$$\sqrt[3]{a^3 + R} \approx a + \frac{R}{3a(a + 1) + 1}.$$

Distinctio VI (Pars I) — De Dimensione Columnarum, Pyramidum et Sperarum
------------------------------------------------------------------------

*Versio Anglica*: :ref:`Distinctio VI Part 1 — Solids & Spheres (pp. 159–190) <unit-4-solids-en>` · *Commentarius*: :ref:`Notae ad Stereometriam <unit-4-s2-notes>`

Pertica corporalis continet pedes cubicos $.CCXVI.$ ($216$). Ex undecimo et duodecimo libro :person:`Euclidis <euclid>` (:cite:`euclid:elements`) atque ex :person:`Archimenide <archimedes>` (:cite:`archimedes:sphere`) demonstratur:

* **Columnae et Prismata**: Mensurantur ex multiplicatione :term:`embadi <embadum>` basis in altitudinem (:term:`cathetum <cathetus>`). :just:`[XII. 11] <XII.11>`
* **Pyramides et Coni**: Omnis pyramis est tertia pars prismatis sive columne habentis eandem basim et eandem altitudinem ($V = \frac{1}{3} B h$). :just:`[XII. 7, 10] <XII.7>`
* **Pyramis Caput Abscisa**: Mensuratur multiplicando tertiam partem catheti in aggregatum embadorum basis $B_1$, capitis $B_2$, et radicis producti unius in alterum ($\sqrt{B_1 B_2}$).
* **Spera**: Omnis spera est **due tertie partes** ($\frac{2}{3}$) cilindri sibi circumscripti, cuius basis dyameter et altitudo equantur dyametro spere $d$; superficies vero spere quadrupla est maximo circulo in ea descripto ($V \approx \frac{11}{21} d^3$).

Distinctio VI (Pars II) — De Quinque Corporibus in Spera Descriptis
-------------------------------------------------------------------

*Versio Anglica*: :ref:`The Five Regular Polyhedra in Sphere Diameter 6 (pp. 190–202) <unit-4-polyhedra-en>` · *Commentarius*: :ref:`Notae ad Euclidis XIII et Paciolum <unit-4-s3-notes>`

.. enunciation::

   Invenire latera et corpulentias quinque corporum regularium tertijdecimi libri :person:`Euclidis <euclid>` in spera cuius dyameter sit perticarum $.VI.$. :just:`[XIII. 13–18] <XIII.18>`

.. picture:: /_static/figures/euclid_xiii_sphere_polyhedra.svg
   :alt: Descriptio Laterum Quinque Corporum Regularium in Spera Dyametri VI
   :caption: Semicirculus ( O A ) super dyametrum spere AI = 6 (centrum O, AK = 2): latus pyramidis / B I / (Q = 24), octaedri / A C / (Q = 18), cubi / A B / (Q = 12), dodecaedri / A N / (Q = 18 - rad. 180) et icosaedri / M I / (Q = 18 - rad. 64 4/5).

Sit dyameter spere $AI = 6$ super lineam :g:`AK <euclid_xiii_sphere_polyhedra:[ A K ]>` circa centrum :g:`O <euclid_xiii_sphere_polyhedra:O>` in circulo :g:`(OA) <euclid_xiii_sphere_polyhedra:( O A )>`, sitque $AK = 2$ tertia pars ipsius $AI$, et erigantur perpendiculares :g:`KB <euclid_xiii_sphere_polyhedra:/ K B />` et :g:`OC <euclid_xiii_sphere_polyhedra:/ O C />`. :just:`[XIII. 18] <XIII.18>`

1. **Pyramis Quatuor Basium Triangularium (Tetraedrum)**: Latus eius est :g:`BI <euclid_xiii_sphere_polyhedra:/ B I />`, cuius quadratum est $AI \cdot KI = 24$ ($e_4 = \sqrt{24}$), cathetus $4$, et corpulentia $\sqrt{192}$ ($8\sqrt{3}$). :just:`[XIII. 13] <XIII.13>`
2. **Cubus Sex Basium Quadratarum**: Latus eius est :g:`AB <euclid_xiii_sphere_polyhedra:/ A B />`, cuius quadratum est $AI \cdot AK = 12$ ($e_6 = \sqrt{12}$), et corpulentia $\sqrt{1{,}728}$ ($24\sqrt{3}$). :just:`[XIII. 15] <XIII.15>`
3. **Octaedrum Octo Basium Triangularium**: Latus eius est :g:`AC <euclid_xiii_sphere_polyhedra:/ A C />`, cuius quadratum est $AO^2 + OC^2 = 18$ ($e_8 = \sqrt{18}$), et corpulentia ex duabus pyramidibus quadratis est numerus rationalis $.XXXVI.$ ($36$). :just:`[XIII. 14] <XIII.14>`

Modus Dividendi Lineam Media et Extrema Proportione: Dodecaedrum et Ycosaedrum
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

*Versio Anglica*: :ref:`Extreme and Mean Ratio: Dodecahedron & Icosahedron (pp. 196–202) <unit-4-dodeca-en>` · *Commentarius*: :ref:`Notae ad Cordam Auream <unit-4-s3-notes>`

.. picture:: /_static/figures/dodecahedron_golden_chord.svg
   :alt: Modus Dividendi Lineam BE Media et Extrema Proportione
   :caption: Divisio corde pentagoni BE = rad. 12 media et extrema proportione per perpendicularem / E C / = rad. 3, circulum ( C E ) et arcum ( B D ), efficiens latus dodecaedri / B G / = / A B / = / A E / = rad. 15 minus rad. 3.

* **Modus Dividendi Lineam Media et Extrema Proportione** (*p. 196*): Quia corda pentagoni dodecaedri est latus cubi $BE = \sqrt{12}$, dividitur $BE$ :term:`media et extrema proportione <media-et-extrema-proportio>` :just:`[VI. 30, XIII. 17] <XIII.17>` secando eam per equalia in :g:`M <dodecahedron_golden_chord:M>`, erigendo per circulum :g:`(EM) <dodecahedron_golden_chord:( E M )>` cathetum :g:`EC <dodecahedron_golden_chord:/ E C />` $= \sqrt{3}$, copulando :g:`BC <dodecahedron_golden_chord:[ B D ]>` ($= \sqrt{15}$), auferendo per circulum :g:`(CE) <dodecahedron_golden_chord:( C E )>` portionem $CD = \sqrt{3}$ in :g:`D <dodecahedron_golden_chord:D>`, et transferendo $BD$ per circulum :g:`(BD) <dodecahedron_golden_chord:( B D )>` in :g:`G <dodecahedron_golden_chord:G>`.
* **Dodecaedrum ($.XII.$ Basium Pentagonalium)**: Maior portio :g:`BG <dodecahedron_golden_chord:/ B G />` (sive :g:`AN <euclid_xiii_sphere_polyhedra:/ A N />` in figura spere) et latera pentagoni :g:`AB <dodecahedron_golden_chord:/ A B />` ac :g:`AE <dodecahedron_golden_chord:/ A E />` in :g:`ABE <dodecahedron_golden_chord:< A B E >>` :eq:`sunt invicem equalia <dodecahedron_golden_chord:/ B G / = dodecahedron_golden_chord:/ A B / = dodecahedron_golden_chord:/ A E />`, linea irrationalis que dicitur :term:`recisum`:

  $$e_{12} = \sqrt{15} - \sqrt{3}, \qquad e_{12}^2 = 18 - \sqrt{180}, \qquad af^2 = 6 - \sqrt{7\frac{1}{5}}, \qquad hf^2 = 3 + \sqrt{7\frac{1}{5}}.$$

* **Ycosaedrum ($.XX.$ Basium Triangularium)**: Per radium :g:`OM <euclid_xiii_sphere_polyhedra:/ O M />` invenitur corda :g:`MI <euclid_xiii_sphere_polyhedra:/ M I />` :just:`[XIII. 16] <XIII.16>`, cuius quadratum est $e_{20}^2 = 18 - \sqrt{64\frac{4}{5}}$; et secundum librum quartumdecimum (:person:`Hypsicles <hypsicles>`) proportio corpulentie dodecaedri ad corpulentiam ycosaedri in eadem spera contentorum est sicut latus cubi ($e_6$) ad latus ycosaedri ($e_{20}$).

.. qed::

   Explicit distinctio sexta in dimensione corporum.
