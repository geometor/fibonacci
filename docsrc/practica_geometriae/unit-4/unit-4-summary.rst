UNIT IV SUMMARY — DISTINCTIO V–VI: SOLIDS & THE FIVE REGULAR POLYHEDRA
======================================================================

.. rubric:: *Structural Synopsis of Boncompagni Vol. II, pp. 148–202 (fol. 92v–130v)*

Overview
--------

Units V and VI ascend from planar fields to three-dimensional bodies: first building the arithmetic of cube roots (*Distinctio V*), then establishing the mensuration of prisms, pyramids, frustums, and spheres (*Distinctio VI*, Part 1), and culminating in the exact quadratic and biquadratic radical invariants of all five regular polyhedra inscribed in a sphere of diameter $d = 6$ (*Distinctio VI*, Part 2).

Exact Invariants of the Five Platonic Solids in Sphere $d = 6$ ($R = 3$)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

.. list-table:: Exact Symbolic Table of the Five Regular Solids (Sphere Diameter $d = 6$)
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 22 26 26 26

   * - Regular Solid
     - Edge Squared $e^2$
     - Exact Edge $e$
     - Exact Solid Volume $V$
   * - **Tetrahedron** ($4$ faces)
     - $\frac{2}{3} d^2 = 24$
     - $2\sqrt{6}$
     - $8\sqrt{3} = \sqrt{192}$
   * - **Cube** ($6$ faces)
     - $\frac{1}{3} d^2 = 12$
     - $2\sqrt{3}$
     - $24\sqrt{3} = \sqrt{1{,}728}$
   * - **Octahedron** ($8$ faces)
     - $\frac{1}{2} d^2 = 18$
     - $3\sqrt{2}$
     - $36$ (*exact integer!*)
   * - **Dodecahedron** ($12$ faces)
     - $18 - 6\sqrt{5} = 18 - \sqrt{180}$
     - $\sqrt{15} - \sqrt{3} = \frac{2\sqrt{3}}{\phi}$
     - $30\sqrt{3} + 6\sqrt{15}$
   * - **Icosahedron** ($20$ faces)
     - $18 - \frac{18}{5}\sqrt{5} = 18 - \sqrt{64\frac{4}{5}}$
     - $\frac{3\sqrt{10 - 2\sqrt{5}}}{\sqrt{5}}$
     - $18\sqrt{10 + 2\sqrt{5}}$
