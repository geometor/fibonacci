Conspectus Totius Operis

*(Analytical Table of Contents)*

UNIT I — PROLOGUE TO MASTER DOMINICUS, PISAN METROLOGY & DISTINCTIO I–II
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

*(Epistola ad Magistrum Dominicum, Introductoria, Distinctio I–II · Boncompagni Vol. II, pp. 1–30, fol. 1r–18v)*

Euclidean definitions and common conceptions, the Pisan :term:`pertica` agrarian system ($6$ :term:`pedes <pes>`, $36$ :term:`denarii <denarius>`, $66$-*pertica* :term:`stariorum`), rectangular field mensuration, Euclid Book II geometric algebra, and iterative square-root extraction.

:ref:`English <unit-1-en>` · :ref:`Latin (1220 / Boncompagni 1862) <unit-1-la>` · :ref:`Summary <unit-1-summary>` · :ref:`Notes <unit-1-notes>`

UNIT II — DISTINCTIO III: MENSURATION OF FIELDS, HERON'S PROOF & CIRCLE BOUNDS
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

*(Distinctio III: In mensuratione omnium camporum · Boncompagni Vol. II, pp. 30–110, fol. 18v–67v)*

Scalene altitudes (:term:`cathetus` and :term:`casus`) on $(13, 14, 15)$, Fibonacci's complete Euclidean incenter proof of :person:`Heron <heron>`'s triangle formula $\Delta = \sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}$, quadrilaterals and polygons, and the small-number 96-gon reformulation of :person:`Archimedes <archimedes>`' circle measurement ($\pi \approx \frac{1440}{458\frac{1}{3}} = \frac{864}{275}$).

:ref:`English <unit-2-en>` · :ref:`Latin (1220 / Boncompagni 1862) <unit-2-la>` · :ref:`Summary <unit-2-summary>` · :ref:`Notes <unit-2-notes>`

UNIT III — DISTINCTIO IV: DIVISION OF PLANAR FIGURES AMONG CONSORTS
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

*(Distinctio IV: De divisione camporum inter consortes · Boncompagni Vol. II, pp. 110–148, fol. 67v–92v)*

Leonardo Pisano's celebrated Latin transmission and expansion of :person:`Euclid <euclid>`'s lost treatise *On Divisions of Figures*: dividing triangles through a vertex, from an arbitrary point on a side, through interior and exterior points, parallel to the base via semicircle mean proportional, into three equal shares at the centroid $X(2)$, and across trapezoids and circles.

:ref:`English <unit-3-en>` · :ref:`Latin (1220 / Boncompagni 1862) <unit-3-la>` · :ref:`Summary <unit-3-summary>` · :ref:`Notes <unit-3-notes>`

UNIT IV — DISTINCTIO V–VI: CUBE ROOTS, SOLIDS & THE FIVE REGULAR POLYHEDRA
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

*(Distinctio V–VI: De radicibus cubicis et dimensione corporum · Boncompagni Vol. II, pp. 148–202, fol. 92v–130v)*

Positional cube-root extraction from $(a+b)^3$, mensuration of prisms, pyramids, cones, frustums, and spheres, and the inscription of all five Platonic solids inside a sphere of diameter $6$ via :term:`extreme and mean ratio <media-et-extrema-proportio>` ($\phi$) and :person:`Hypsicles <hypsicles>`' Book XIV—the direct source of :person:`Piero della Francesca <piero-della-francesca>` and :person:`Luca Pacioli <pacioli>`.

:ref:`English <unit-4-en>` · :ref:`Latin (1220 / Boncompagni 1862) <unit-4-la>` · :ref:`Summary <unit-4-summary>` · :ref:`Notes <unit-4-notes>`

UNIT V — DISTINCTIO VII–VIII, *FLOS* (1225) & *LIBER ABACI* CONVERGENCE TO $\phi$
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

*(Distinctio VII–VIII, Flos & Liber Abaci Cap. XII · Boncompagni Vol. II, pp. 202–247 & Vol. I, pp. 283–284)*

Optical surveying of heights and depths with the :term:`quadrans` shadow square, *Subtilitates Geometricae* inscribing the pentagon and decagon in diameter $10$ by :person:`al-Khwārizmī <al-khwarizmi>`'s and :person:`Abū Kāmil <abu-kamil>`'s *census census* quartic ($x^4 + 3125 = 125x^2$), the *Flos* sexagesimal cubic ($x^3 + 2x^2 + 10x = 20$), and the *Liber Abaci* rabbit recurrence $F_{n+1} = F_n + F_{n-1}$ converging to $\phi$.

:ref:`English <unit-5-en>` · :ref:`Latin (1220 / Boncompagni 1862) <unit-5-la>` · :ref:`Summary <unit-5-summary>` · :ref:`Notes <unit-5-notes>`
