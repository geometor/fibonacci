Glossary
========

.. rubric:: *Lexicon of Pisan Agrarian Metrology, Euclidean Geometry, and Graeco-Arabic Algebra*

Every inline ``:term:`` tag across ``geometor-fibonacci`` links directly to its ``#term-<slug>`` anchor below.

----

.. _term-pertica:

pertica (pertica pisana) — Pisan surveying rod
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* **Tradition**: Medieval Latin (Pisan Metrology)
* **English Rendering**: *Pisan surveying rod (linear or superficial)*

Fundamental Pisan unit of land measurement: :math:`1` linear *pertica* :math:`= 6` linear *pedes* :math:`= 108` *unciae* (*puncta*); :math:`1` superficial (square) *pertica* :math:`= 6` superficial *pedes* :math:`= 36` *denarii* :math:`= 108` superficial *unciae* (*Practica Geometriae*, *Introductoria*, p. 3).

.. _term-pes:

pes (linealis / superficialis) — Pisan foot
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* **Tradition**: Medieval Latin (Pisan Metrology)
* **English Rendering**: Pisan foot (:math:`1/6` of a pertica)

As a linear measure, :math:`1/6` of a *pertica* (:math:`= 18` *unciae*). As a superficial strip (*pes terrae*), a rectangle :math:`1` *pertica* (:math:`6` linear *pedes*) long by :math:`1` linear *pes* wide, equal to :math:`1/6` of a square *pertica* or :math:`6` *denarii* (*Introductoria*, p. 3).

.. _term-uncia:

uncia / punctum — inch or point
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* **Tradition**: Medieval Latin (Pisan Metrology)
* **English Rendering**: inch or point (:math:`1/18` of a Pisan pes; :math:`1/108` of a pertica)

Fine subdivision of the Pisan rod: :math:`18` linear *unciae* make :math:`1` linear *pes*, and :math:`108` linear *unciae* make :math:`1` *pertica*. Superficially, :math:`18` superficial *unciae* make :math:`1` superficial *pes* (*Introductoria*, p. 3).

.. _term-denarius:

denarius de mensura — measuring denarius
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* **Tradition**: Medieval Latin (Pisan Metrology)
* **English Rendering**: measuring denarius (:math:`1` square pes :math:`= 1/36` square pertica)

Unit square of side :math:`1` linear *pes* inside the :math:`6 \times 6` grid of the square *pertica*, so that :math:`6` *denarii* equal :math:`1` superficial *pes* and :math:`36` *denarii* equal :math:`1` superficial *pertica* (*Distinctio I*, pp. 3–5).

.. _term-panorum:

panorum — Pisan panorum (:math:`5\frac{1}{2}` square perticae)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* **Tradition**: Medieval Latin (Pisan Metrology)
* **English Rendering**: panorum (:math:`5\frac{1}{2}` square perticae)

Pisan agrarian land measure equal to :math:`5\frac{1}{2}` superficial *perticae* (:math:`= 1/12` of a *stariorum*), used throughout the episcopate of Pisa (*Introductoria*, p. 3).

.. _term-stariorum:

stariorum — Pisan stariorum (:math:`66` square perticae)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* **Tradition**: Medieval Latin (Pisan Metrology)
* **English Rendering**: stariorum (:math:`12` panora :math:`= 66` square perticae)

Principal Pisan grain-seeding field area equal to :math:`12` *panora* or :math:`66` superficial *perticae*; :math:`24` *stariora* constitute :math:`1` *modiorum* (:math:`= 1584` square *perticae*) (*Introductoria*, pp. 3–5).

.. _term-embadum:

embadum — planar area / surface content
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* **Tradition**: Medieval Latin (from Greek *embadon* via Arabic/Hebrew)
* **English Rendering**: *planar area / surface content (or squared side)*

Standard Graeco-Latin technical term across Savasorda and Fibonacci for the superficial content (area) of a plane figure, and by extension the square built on the edge of a polyhedron (*embadum lateris*, *Distinctio III & VI*).

.. _term-cathetus:

cathetus — perpendicular altitude
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* **Tradition**: Latin (from Greek *kathetos*)
* **English Rendering**: *perpendicular altitude*

Perpendicular line dropped from a vertex of a triangle, pyramid, or cone onto the opposite base line or base plane (*linea perpendicularis, quae cathetus appellatur*, *Distinctio III*, p. 39).

.. _term-casus:

casus (maior / minor) — base projection segment
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* **Tradition**: Medieval Latin
* **English Rendering**: *projection segment cut on the base by the altitude*

Each of the two segments into which the foot of the *cathetus* divides the base of a triangle; on the :math:`(13, 14, 15)` triangle with base :math:`14`, the minor *casus* is :math:`5`, the major *casus* is :math:`9`, and the *cathetus* is :math:`12` (*Distinctio III*, p. 39).

.. _term-consortes:

consortes — co-heirs / landholding partners
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* **Tradition**: Latin (Legal & Agrarian)
* **English Rendering**: *co-heirs / joint landholding partners*

Partners or co-owners sharing a field, orchard, well, or boundary access in fixed rational shares (e.g. halves, thirds, or :math:`p : q`), motivating the exact Euclidean division constructions of *Distinctio IV* (*de divisione camporum inter consortes*, p. 110).

.. _term-media-et-extrema-proportio:

media et extrema proportio — extreme and mean ratio (:math:`\phi`)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* **Tradition**: Latin (Euclid VI.def.3 / II.11 / XIII.1–18)
* **English Rendering**: extreme and mean ratio (the golden section :math:`\phi`)

Division of a segment :math:`BE` at :math:`G` so that the whole :math:`BE` is to the greater part :math:`BG` as :math:`BG` is to the lesser part :math:`GE` (:math:`BE : BG = BG : GE = \phi = \frac{1 + \sqrt{5}}{2}`). Used by Fibonacci in *Distinctio VI* (pp. 196–202) to construct the dodecahedron and icosahedron and in *Distinctio VIII* (pp. 207–208) for the pentagon and decagon.

.. _term-res:

res / radix — algebraic unknown (:math:`x`)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* **Tradition**: Medieval Latin (from Arabic *shayʾ* / *jidhr*)
* **English Rendering**: the unknown quantity :math:`x` ('thing' or 'root')

First-degree algebraic unknown :math:`x` in al-Khwārizmī's and Fibonacci's rhetorical algebra (*Distinctio VIII*, pp. 207–224; *Liber Abaci* Cap. XV).

.. _term-census:

census — square of the unknown (:math:`x^2`)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* **Tradition**: Medieval Latin (from Arabic *māl*)
* **English Rendering**: the square of the unknown (:math:`x^2`)

Second-degree algebraic power :math:`x^2` ('wealth/square'); doubled in *Distinctio VIII* as *census census* for the fourth power :math:`x^4` in biquadratic polygon problems such as :math:`x^4 + 3125 = 125 x^2` (pp. 207–208).

.. _term-binomiale:

linea binomialis / binomiale — binomial irrational
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* **Tradition**: Medieval Latin (Euclid Book X.36)
* **English Rendering**: binomial irrational line (:math:`a + \sqrt{b}` or :math:`\sqrt{a} + \sqrt{b}`)

Compound irrational magnitude formed by adding two lines commensurable in square only, classified into six species in Euclid Book X and tested systematically by Fibonacci in *Distinctio VIII* and *Flos*.

.. _term-recisum:

recisum (apotome) — apotome / residual irrational
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* **Tradition**: Medieval Latin (from Gerard of Cremona's Euclid Book X.73)
* **English Rendering**: apotome / residual irrational line (:math:`a - \sqrt{b}` or :math:`\sqrt{a} - \sqrt{b}`)

Irrational difference of two lines commensurable in square only—exemplified by the dodecahedron edge :math:`\sqrt{15} - \sqrt{3}` (a fourth apotome in a sphere of rational diameter :math:`6`, *Distinctio VI*, p. 196) and the decagon chord :math:`\sqrt{31\frac{1}{4}} - 2\frac{1}{2}` (*Distinctio VIII*, p. 208).

.. _term-congruum:

congruum — common step of three squares in arithmetic progression
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* **Tradition**: Medieval Latin (Fibonacci, *Flos* & *Liber Quadratorum*, 1225)
* **English Rendering**: *congruum (common difference of three squares in arithmetic progression)*

An integer :math:`D = 4ab(a^2 - b^2)` (always divisible by :math:`24`) such that :math:`z^2 - D`, :math:`z^2`, and :math:`z^2 + D` are simultaneously squares. Dividing by :math:`12^2` yields the rational triple :math:`(\frac{31}{12})^2, (\frac{41}{12})^2, (\frac{49}{12})^2` with common difference :math:`5` (*Flos*, p. 227), and connects geometrically to trapezoid bisection :math:`c^2 = (a^2 + b^2)/2` in *Distinctio IV*.

.. _term-quadrans:

quadrans (oroscopus) — surveyor's quadrant with shadow square
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* **Tradition**: Medieval Latin
* **English Rendering**: *surveyor's and astronomer's quadrant with shadow square*

Quarter-circle sighting instrument equipped with two pinnules (*tabulae perforatae*), a plumb bob (*perpendiculum*), a 90-degree arc, and an inscribed :math:`12 \times 12` shadow square (*umbra recta* / *umbra versa*) used in *Distinctio VII* (p. 204) to measure inaccessible tower heights and valley widths by similar right triangles.
