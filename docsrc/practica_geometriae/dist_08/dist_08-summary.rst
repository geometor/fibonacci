The Algebraic Quartic of Abū Kāmil
----------------------------------

* **Equation**: $x^4 + 3{,}125 = 125\,x^2$.
* **Roots in Census ($x^2$)**:
  
  $$x^2 = \frac{125 \pm \sqrt{125^2 - 4 \times 3{,}125}}{2} = 62\frac{1}{2} \pm \sqrt{781\frac{1}{4}}.$$

  The lesser root gives the pentagon side $s_5^2 = 62\frac{1}{2} - \sqrt{781\frac{1}{4}}$; the greater root gives the diagonal $d_5^2 = 62\frac{1}{2} + \sqrt{781\frac{1}{4}}$.
