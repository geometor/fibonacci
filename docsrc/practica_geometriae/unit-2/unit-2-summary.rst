UNIT II SUMMARY — DISTINCTIO III: PLANAR MENSURATION & CIRCLE BOUNDS
====================================================================

.. rubric:: *Structural Synopsis of Boncompagni Vol. II, pp. 30–110 (fol. 18v–67v)*

:ref:`English Edition <unit-2-en>` · :ref:`Latin (1220 / Boncompagni 1862) <unit-2-la>` · :ref:`GEOMETOR Notes <unit-2-notes>`

Overview
--------

*Distinctio III* is the longest mensuration book of *Practica Geometriae*, advancing systematically from triangles to quadrilaterals, regular and irregular polygons, and finally circular circumference, area, sectors, and segments.

Core Propositions & Numerical Benchmarks
----------------------------------------

.. list-table:: Canonical Constructions & Exact Invariants in Distinctio III
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 28 36 36

   * - Section / Folio
     - Geometric Problem
     - Exact Result in *Practica Geometriae*
   * - **Scalene Altitude** (:ref:`fol. 24r <unit-2-cathetus-en>`)
     - *Casus* & *cathetus* of $\triangle(13, 14, 15)$
     - $bd = 5$, $dg = 9$, $ad = 12$, $\Delta = 84$
   * - **Heron's Formula** (:ref:`fol. 24v–25v <unit-2-heron-en>`)
     - Area from $(a, b, c)$ via incenter $T$ & semiperimeter $s$
     - $\Delta^2 = r^2 s^2 = s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c) = 7{,}056$
   * - **Quadrilaterals** (:ref:`fol. 34r–53v <unit-2-quad-en>`)
     - Rhombi, trapezoids (*caput abscisum*), irregular 4-gons
     - Triangulation via measured diagonal; refutes false $(a+c)(b+d)/4$
   * - **Circumscribed 96-gon** (:ref:`fol. 54r <unit-2-circle-en>`)
     - 4 bisections of $30^\circ$ tangent at $r = 720$ ($d = 1440$)
     - $\pi < \frac{1440}{458\frac{1}{5}} = \frac{7200}{2291} \approx 3.142732$
   * - **Inscribed 96-gon** (:ref:`fol. 55r <unit-2-circle-en>`)
     - 4 bisections of hexagon chord in semicircle $d = 1440$
     - $\pi > \frac{1440}{458\frac{4}{9}} = \frac{6480}{2063} \approx 3.141057$
   * - **Fibonacci's Mean $\pi$** (:ref:`fol. 56r <unit-2-circle-en>`)
     - Intermediate divisor $458\frac{1}{3}$ & reduction to $\frac{22}{7}$
     - $\frac{1440}{458\frac{1}{3}} = \frac{864}{275} = 3\frac{39}{275} \approx 3.141818$
