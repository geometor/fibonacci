Canonical Problem: Laying Out One Stariorum
-------------------------------------------

* **Given**: A plot of land whose area must equal exactly **one Pisan stariorum** (:math:`\Delta = 66` superficial *perticae*), with a fixed frontage of :math:`w = 7` *perticae*.
* **Construct**: Divide :math:`66` by :math:`7`:

  .. math::

     \text{Depth} = \frac{66}{7} = 9\frac{3}{7} \text{ perticae}.

* **Demonstrate**: In Pisan subdivision:

  .. math::

     \frac{3}{7} \text{ pertica} = \frac{18}{7} \text{ pedes} = 2 \text{ pedes} + \frac{4}{7} \text{ pes} = 2 \text{ pedes} + \frac{72}{7} \text{ unciae} = 2\text{p } 10\frac{2}{7}\text{in}.

  Thus the exact dimensions are :math:`7 \text{ perticae} \times \left(9\text{p } 2\text{ft } 10\frac{2}{7}\text{in}\right) = 66 \text{ perticae}`.
