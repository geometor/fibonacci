Overview
--------

*Distinctio IV* preserves the fullest Latin witness to :person:`Euclid <euclid>`'s lost Greek treatise *On Divisions of Figures* ($\Pi\varepsilon\rho\grave{\iota}\ \delta\iota\alpha\iota\rho\acute{\varepsilon}\sigma\varepsilon\omega\nu\ \beta\iota\beta\lambda\acute{\iota}\omicron\nu$, :cite:`euclid:divisions`), systematically augmenting every pure ruler-and-compass theorem with numerical coordinates and quadratic solutions for Pisan estate partitioning among :term:`consortes`.

Catalogue of Partition Constructions
------------------------------------

.. list-table:: Consortium Partition Constructions in Distinctio IV
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 28 36 36

   * - Problem Class
     - Geometric Construction
     - Canonical Example & Invariant
   * - **Vertex Partition** (:ref:`fol. 67v <unit-3-sidepoint-en>`)
     - Divide opposite base $BG$ in ratio $p : q$, join to $A$
     - $\triangle ABD : \triangle ADG = BD : DG$ (Euclid VI.1)
   * - **Boundary Point $D$** (:ref:`fol. 68v <unit-3-sidepoint-en>`)
     - Draw $EZ \parallel AD$ from midpoint $E$, cut along $DZ$
     - On $(13, 14, 15)$ with $BD=5, DG=9$: $GZ = \frac{35}{3} = 11\frac{2}{3}$
   * - **Centroid $X(2)$** (:ref:`fol. 69r, 74r <unit-3-centroid-en>`)
     - Concurrency of medians $AE, BZ, GT$ in $2 : 1$ ratio at $D$
     - Three equal shares $\triangle DAB = \triangle DBG = \triangle DGA = \frac{1}{3}\Delta$
   * - **Interior / Exterior $P$** (:ref:`fol. 69v–72r <unit-3-centroid-en>`)
     - Application of areas (Euclid VI.28–29) & Book II quadratics
     - Line through arbitrary landmark $P$ cutting off $\lambda \Delta$
   * - **Parallel to Base** (:ref:`fol. 73v <unit-3-parallel-en>`)
     - Semicircle mean proportional $AE^2 = \lambda\, AB^2$ (Euclid VI.13, 19)
     - On $(10, 14, 12)$ for $\lambda = \frac{1}{2}$: $AE^2 = 50, AZ^2 = 72, EZ^2 = 98$
   * - **Trapezoid Parallel Cut** (:ref:`fol. 78r <unit-3-trapezoid-en>`)
     - Weighted root-mean-square of parallel bases $a, b$
     - $c^2 = \frac{q a^2 + p b^2}{p + q}$; for $(7, 17)$, $c = 13$
   * - **Circles & Segments** (:ref:`fol. 90r–92v <unit-3-trapezoid-en>`)
     - Concentric rings ($r_k^2 = \frac{k}{n} R^2$) & chord partitions
     - Euclid *On Divisions* Props. 28–29
