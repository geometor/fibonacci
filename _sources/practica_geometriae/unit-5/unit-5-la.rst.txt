Distinctio VII — De Inventione Altitudinum Rerum Elevatarum et Quadrante
------------------------------------------------------------------------

*Versio Anglica*: :ref:`Distinctio VII — Surveying & the Quadrans (pp. 202–207) <unit-5-dist7-en>` · *Commentarius*: :ref:`Notae ad Quadrantem <unit-5-s1-notes>`

.. enunciation::

   Explicit distinctio sexta; incipit septima distinctio de inventione altitudinum rerum elevatarum, profunditatum atque planorum.

.. picture:: /_static/figures/quadrans_surveying.svg
   :alt: Quadrans Geometricus (Quem Quidem Oroscopum Vocant)
   :caption: Quadrans < D H A I > habens in utroque latere (umbra recta [ I P ] et umbra versa [ A H ]) puncta duodecim, cum quarta circuli ( D I ), dyametro [ A B ] et perpendiculo / D P / secante in P = 8.

Ostenditur mensuratio altitudinum tam per virgas et specula ex similitudine trigonorum rectangulorum :just:`[VI. 4] <VI.4>`, quam per instrumentum quod dicitur :term:`quadrans` (*quem quidem oroscopum vocant*, *p. 204*). Intra quartam partem circuli :g:`(DI) <quadrans_surveying:( D I )>` describitur quadratum :g:`DHAI <quadrans_surveying:< D H A I >>`, cuius utrumque latus :g:`IA <quadrans_surveying:[ I P ]>` (*umbra recta*) et :g:`AH <quadrans_surveying:[ A H ]>` (*umbra versa*) dividitur in **duodecim puncta equalia**, protractaque dyametro :g:`DA <quadrans_surveying:[ A B ]>` usque in :g:`B <quadrans_surveying:B>`. Cum autem filum perpendiculi :g:`DP <quadrans_surveying:/ D P />` (secans arcum per :g:`DC <quadrans_surveying:[ D C ]>` in :g:`C <quadrans_surveying:C>`) ceciderit super punctum :g:`P <quadrans_surveying:P>` (utpote super $.VIII.$ puncta), proportio spatij plani ad altitudinem rei super oculum mensoris est sicut $.VIII.$ ad $.XII.$.

Distinctio VIII — De Quibusdam Subtilitatibus Geometricis
---------------------------------------------------------

*Versio Anglica*: :ref:`Distinctio VIII — Pentagon, Decagon & Al-Jabr (pp. 207–226) <unit-5-dist8-en>` · *Commentarius*: :ref:`Notae ad Censum Census <unit-5-s2-notes>`

.. enunciation::

   Incipit distinctio octava de quibusdam subtilitatibus geometricis: invenire latus decagoni et pentagoni equilateri in circulo cuius dyameter sit $.X.$.

.. picture:: /_static/figures/pentagon_decagon_circle.svg
   :alt: Descriptio Pentagoni et Decagoni in Circulo Dyametri X
   :caption: Circulus ( I A ) dyametri CA = 10 circa centrum I: per punctum medium H et spatium / H D / = rad. 31 1/4 describitur arcus ( H D ) determinans latus decagoni / I K / = / D Q1 / et latus pentagoni / D K / in figura < D P1 P2 P3 P4 >.

In circulo :g:`(IA) <pentagon_decagon_circle:( I A )>` dyametri $CA = 10$ per :g:`C <pentagon_decagon_circle:C>`, :g:`I <pentagon_decagon_circle:I>`, :g:`A <pentagon_decagon_circle:A>`, ducatur semidyameter perpendicularis :g:`ID <pentagon_decagon_circle:/ I D />` $= 5$, dividaturque $IC$ per equalia in :g:`H <pentagon_decagon_circle:H>` ($2\frac{1}{2}$), ita ut quadratum :g:`HD <pentagon_decagon_circle:/ H D />` sit $31\frac{1}{4}$. Descripto circulo :g:`(HD) <pentagon_decagon_circle:( H D )>` secante dyametrum :g:`IK <pentagon_decagon_circle:[ I K ]>` in :g:`K <pentagon_decagon_circle:K>`, erit :g:`IK <pentagon_decagon_circle:/ I K />` :eq:`latus decagoni equale corde <pentagon_decagon_circle:/ I K / = pentagon_decagon_circle:/ D Q1 / = pentagon_decagon_circle:/ Q1 P1 />` :g:`DQ1 <pentagon_decagon_circle:/ D Q1 />`: :just:`[XIII. 9] <XIII.9>`

$$IK = \sqrt{31\frac{1}{4}} - 2\frac{1}{2};$$

et subtensa :g:`DK <pentagon_decagon_circle:/ D K />` erit latus pentagoni :g:`DP1P2P3P4 <pentagon_decagon_circle:< D P1 P2 P3 P4 >>` (:g:`P1 <pentagon_decagon_circle:P1>`, :g:`P2 <pentagon_decagon_circle:P2>`, :g:`P3 <pentagon_decagon_circle:P3>`, :g:`P4 <pentagon_decagon_circle:P4>`): :just:`[XIII. 10] <XIII.10>`

$$DK = \sqrt{62\frac{1}{2} - \sqrt{781\frac{1}{4}}}.$$

Idemque demonstratur secundum regulam algebre (:person:`Maumeti <al-khwarizmi>` et :person:`Abu Camil <abu-kamil>`), ponendo latus pentagoni :term:`rem <res>` unam ($x$), unde **census census** et $3{,}125$ equantur $125$ :term:`censibus <census>` ($x^4 + 3{,}125 = 125 x^2$).

Appendix I — Ex *Flore* Leonardi Pisani (Anno M.CC.XXV)
-------------------------------------------------------

*Versio Anglica*: :ref:`Bridge I — Flos: The Imperial Cubic & Congruum (pp. 227–234) <unit-5-flos-en>` · *Commentarius*: :ref:`Notae ad Florem <unit-5-s3-notes>`

Coram serenissimo imperatore :person:`Frederico secundo <frederick-ii>`, magister :person:`Iohannes Panormitanus <john-of-palermo>` proposuit duas questiones (:cite:`fibonacci:flos`):

1. **De Congruo Quinarii**: Invenire numerum quadratum cui si addatur vel minuatur quinarius, semper oriatur numerus quadratus. Per :term:`congruum` $720 = 5 \times 144$ ostenditur radicem quesitam esse $3\frac{5}{12} = \frac{41}{12}$, cum $\left(\frac{41}{12}\right)^2 - 5 = \left(\frac{31}{12}\right)^2$ et $\left(\frac{41}{12}\right)^2 + 5 = \left(\frac{49}{12}\right)^2$.
2. **De Cubo et Censibus ac Rebus**: Invenire lineam ex qua **cubus et duo census et decem radices equentur viginti** ($x^3 + 2x^2 + 10x = 20$). Demonstrato per decimum librum :person:`Euclidis <euclid>` quod hec radix neque rationalis est neque ulla ex lineis irrationalibus decimi libri (:term:`binomiale`, :term:`recisum`, medialis), invenit Leonardus eius propinquationem secundum minutias astronomorum:

   $$x \approx 1^{\circ}\ 22'\ 7''\ 42'''\ 33''''\ 4'''''\ 40''''''.$$

Appendix II — Ex *Libro Abbaci* Cap. XII: Quot Paria Coniculorum
----------------------------------------------------------------

*Versio Anglica*: :ref:`Bridge II — Liber Abaci Rabbit Recurrence (Vol. I, pp. 283–284) <unit-5-rabbits-en>` · *Commentarius*: :ref:`Notae ad Seriem Leonardi <unit-5-s4-notes>`

.. enunciation::

   Quot paria coniculorum in uno anno ex uno pario germinentur (:cite:`boncompagni:1857`, *p. 283*).

Quidam posuit unum par coniculorum in quodam loco, qui erat undique pariete circundatus, ut sciret quot ex eo paria germinarentur in uno anno: cum natura eorum sit in uno mense aliud par germinare, et in secundo mense ab eorum nativitate germinant. Cum in primo sint $2$, in secundo $3$, in tertio $5$, in quarto $8$, in quinto $13$, in sexto $21$, in septimo $34$, in octavo $55$, in nono $89$, in decimo $144$, in undecimo $233$, et in duodecimo **$377$**.

.. qed::

   Iunximus namque primum numerum cum secundo, videlicet $1$ cum $2$; et secundum cum tertio; et tertium cum quarto; et quartum cum quinto; et sic unum post aliud, donec iunximus decimum cum undecimo, videlicet $144$ cum $233$; et habuimus suprascriptorum coniculorum summam, videlicet $377$; **et sic posses facere per ordinem de infinitis numeris mensibus**.
