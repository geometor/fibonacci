UNIT V SUMMARY — DISTINCTIO VII–VIII, *FLOS* & *LIBER ABACI*
============================================================

.. rubric:: *Structural Synopsis of Boncompagni Vol. II, pp. 202–247 & Vol. I, pp. 283–284*

:ref:`English Edition <unit-5-en>` · :ref:`Latin (1220 / Boncompagni 1862) <unit-5-la>` · :ref:`GEOMETOR Notes <unit-5-notes>`

----

Overview
--------

Unit V completes *Practica Geometriae* with optical trigonometry (*Distinctio VII*) and algebraic-geometric *subtilitates* (*Distinctio VIII*), then bridges directly to Leonardo's two sister masterpieces—*Flos* ($1225$) and *Liber Abaci* ($1202/1228$)—exhibiting the deep unity of Pisan mensuration, Islamic *al-jabr*, Euclidean irrationals, and the golden ratio $\phi$.

Summary of Propositions & Exact Values
--------------------------------------

.. list-table:: Optical, Algebraic, Cubic & Golden Convergents in Unit V
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 26 37 37

   * - Locus
     - Problem / Construction
     - Exact Symbolic or Sexagesimal Form
   * - **Quadrans** (:ref:`Dist. VII <unit-5-dist7-en>`)
     - Tower elevation from $12 \times 12$ *umbra recta* / *versa*
     - $H = h_{\text{eye}} + \frac{12}{u_r} D$ (for $u_r = 8, D = 40$: $H = 61$)
   * - **Decagon in $d = 10$** (:ref:`Dist. VIII <unit-5-dist8-en>`)
     - Golden section of hexagon side $r = 5$ (Euclid XIII.9)
     - $s_{10} = \sqrt{31\frac{1}{4}} - 2\frac{1}{2} = \frac{5}{\phi}$
   * - **Pentagon in $d = 10$** (:ref:`Dist. VIII <unit-5-dist8-en>`)
     - Euclid XIII.10 & *census census* $x^4 + 3125 = 125x^2$
     - $s_5 = \sqrt{62\frac{1}{2} - \sqrt{781\frac{1}{4}}} = \frac{5\sqrt{10 - 2\sqrt{5}}}{2}$
   * - **Congruum of $5$** (:ref:`Flos <unit-5-flos-en>`)
     - Three rational squares in arithmetic progression of step $5$
     - $\left(\frac{31}{12}\right)^2,\ \left(\frac{41}{12}\right)^2,\ \left(\frac{49}{12}\right)^2$
   * - **Imperial Cubic** (:ref:`Flos <unit-5-flos-en>`)
     - $x^3 + 2x^2 + 10x = 20$ ($\notin$ Euclid Book X)
     - $1; 22, 7, 42, 33, 4, 40_{60} = \frac{1{,}596{,}577{,}777}{1{,}166{,}400{,}000}$
   * - **Rabbit Sequence** (:ref:`Liber Abaci XII <unit-5-rabbits-en>`)
     - $F_{n+1} = F_n + F_{n-1}$ ($1, 2, 3, \dots, 377$)
     - $\lim_{n\to\infty} \frac{F_{n+1}}{F_n} = \phi$, $F_{n+1}F_{n-1} - F_n^2 = (-1)^n$
