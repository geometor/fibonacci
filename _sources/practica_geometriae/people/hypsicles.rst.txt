Hypsicles of Alexandria
=======================

**Role**: *Author of Elements Book XIV*

Alexandrian geometer whose treatise (*Book XIV* of the Euclidean corpus) proved that the dodecahedron and icosahedron inscribed in the same sphere share an identical face circumcircle and satisfy :math:`V_{12} : V_{20} = S_{12} : S_{20} = e_6 : e_{20}`—explicitly cited and demonstrated by Fibonacci at the close of *Distinctio VI* (pp. 197–202).
