Taxonomy of Field Division Constructions
----------------------------------------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 25 35 40

   * - Division Problem
     - Geometric Method
     - Exact Metric / Coordinate Solution
   * - **Vertex Partition**
     - Ratio of opposite base [VI. 1]
     - $\Delta_1 : \Delta_2 = b_1 : b_2$
   * - **Boundary Point $D$**
     - Parallel transversal [I. 37]
     - $GZ = \frac{BG \cdot AG}{2 \cdot DG} = \frac{14 \times 15}{2 \times 9} = \frac{35}{3} = 11\frac{2}{3}$
   * - **Centroid $X(2)$**
     - Concurrency of three medians
     - $D = \left(\frac{19}{3}, 4\right)$, $\Delta_1 = \Delta_2 = \Delta_3 = 28$
   * - **Base Parallel Cut**
     - Semicircle mean proportional [VI. 19]
     - $AE^2 = 50$, $AZ^2 = 72$, $EZ^2 = 98$
   * - **Trapezoid RMS Cut**
     - Duplicate ratio of apex completion
     - $c = \sqrt{\frac{a^2 + b^2}{2}} = \sqrt{\frac{49 + 289}{2}} = 13$
   * - **Circles & Portions**
     - Concentric radius or sector bisection
     - $r_{\text{cut}} = \frac{R}{\sqrt{2}}$

Canonical Problem: Side-Point Division on $(13, 14, 15)$
--------------------------------------------------------

* **Given**: Scalene triangle $ABG$ ($ab = 13, bg = 14, ag = 15$, area $84$) and boundary point $D$ on base $BG$ with $BD = 5, DG = 9$.
* **Construct**: Draw $BT \parallel AD$ meeting $AG$ produced at $T$. Bisect $TG$ at $Z$. Draw cut line $DZ$.
* **Demonstrate**: $\Delta(DZG) = \frac{1}{2} \Delta(TDG) = \frac{1}{2} \Delta(ABG) = 42$, leaving quadrilateral $ABDZ = 42$.
