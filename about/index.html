Extracting Historical Semantic Knowledge & Repurposing It for Exact Symbolic Reasoning

About This Edition¶

GEOMETOR fibonacci reconstructs the mathematical corpus of Leonardo Pisano (c. 1170 – c. 1245), widely known as Fibonacci, focusing primarily on his monumental 1220 treatise Practica Geometriae alongside foundational passages from Liber Abaci (1202/1228) and the cubic solution in Flos (1225). Writing in medieval Pisa after traveling throughout North Africa, Egypt, Syria, and Greece, Leonardo synthesized Arabic algebraic techniques with Euclidean axiomatic rigor. His work preserves the primary Latin transmission of Euclid’s lost monograph On Divisions of Figures (Distinctio IV), Archimedean polygon-bounding techniques for circle quadrature, Heron’s incenter proof of triangular area, and Euclidean regular polyhedra inscriptions linked to the golden ratio.

Every distinctio, field division case, surveying problem, and geometric construction has been extracted into addressable semantic elements. Grounded in Baldassarre Boncompagni’s nineteenth-century critical Latin text (Scritti di Leonardo Pisano, Vol. I, 1857; Vol. II, 1862), each unit is presented across four synchronized tracks: diplomatic Latin (littera), an original GEOMETOR English translation, a pedagogical summary, and detailed scholarly notes. Historical geometric figures have been reconstructed as calibrated, resolution-independent vector graphics, and every calculation—from Pisan agrarian metrology to polynomial approximations—is verified to exact symbolic truth in sympy.

This edition forms a crucial connective nexus across the GEOMETOR library. It directly bridges the classical Greek foundations of Euclid's Elements and Heron of Alexandria’s Metrica with the Renaissance resurgence of perspective and golden ratio geometry in Piero della Francesca and Luca Pacioli’s De Divina Proportione (1509). By transforming medieval algorithmic geometry into executable symbolic workflows, this edition recovers the deductive lineage uniting ancient Greece, medieval Islam, and the Italian Renaissance.

The GEOMETOR Mission¶

Founded and directed by phi ARCHITECT, the GEOMETOR initiative is dedicated to the systematic digitization, symbolic formalization, and multi-medium re-articulation of classical geometric and axiomatic literature across 2,300+ years of mathematical history. GEOMETOR bridges historical philology and modern computational geometry through three structural pillars:

Extracting Historical Semantic Knowledge

Moving beyond passive page scans and lossy optical character recognition (OCR), GEOMETOR recovers the living deductive architecture of classical, Renaissance, and modern mathematical treatises. Enunciations, geometric constructions, proof steps, axiomatic justifications, marginal citations, primary-source folios (rendered dynamically through publish folio), and calibrated Ink-to-Vector .euclid-graphic diagrams are converted into structured, machine-addressable semantic graphs.

Exact Symbolic Grounding (geometor.model)

Eliminating floating-point approximation errors entirely, GEOMETOR anchors geometric constructions and center proofs in 100% exact symbolic algebra via sympy and Rational Trigonometry invariants (quadrance, spread, cross-ratio) evaluated over exact algebraic number fields. Every center, line concurrence, circle intersection, and tangency condition is verified to exact symbolic zero.

Repurposing Knowledge Across Mediums

Extracted semantic and algebraic models are mobilized across synchronized multi-track Book Codices and EPUB 3 editions via photon-platform/publish, computable deductive canons (geometor.elements, geometor.etc, geometor.pappus, geometor.divine), interactive SVG laboratories (geometor.explorer), high-definition visual broadcasts, and neuro-symbolic program synthesis engines (geometor.seer) across geometor.com.
