John of Palermo¶

Role: Imperial Court Philosopher & Arabic-Latin Translator

Philosopher at Frederick II’s court who challenged Leonardo Pisano with the cubic equation $x^3 + 2x^2 + 10x = 20$ and the Diophantine congruum problem $x^2 \pm 5 = \square$, answered in Flos and Liber Quadratorum (1225).
