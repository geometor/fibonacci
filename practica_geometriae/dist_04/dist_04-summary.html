Canonical Problem: Side-Point Division on $(13, 14, 15)$¶

  • Given: Scalene triangle $ABG$ ($ab = 13, bg = 14, ag = 15$, area $84$) and boundary point $D$ on base $BG$ with $BD = 5, DG = 9$.

  • Construct: Draw $BT \parallel AD$ meeting $AG$ produced at $T$. Bisect $TG$ at $Z$. Draw cut line $DZ$.

  • Demonstrate: $\Delta(DZG) = \frac{1}{2} \Delta(TDG) = \frac{1}{2} \Delta(ABG) = 42$, leaving quadrilateral $ABDZ = 42$.
