UNIT III SUMMARY — DISTINCTIO IV: DIVISIONS OF PLANAR FIGURES¶

Structural Synopsis of Boncompagni Vol. II, pp. 110–148 (fol. 67v–92v)

English Edition · Latin (1220 / Boncompagni 1862) · GEOMETOR Notes

Overview¶

Distinctio IV preserves the fullest Latin witness to Euclid’s lost Greek treatise On Divisions of Figures ($\Pi\varepsilon\rho\grave{\iota}\ \delta\iota\alpha\iota\rho\acute{\varepsilon}\sigma\varepsilon\omega\nu\ \beta\iota\beta\lambda\acute{\iota}\omicron\nu$, euclid:divisions), systematically augmenting every pure ruler-and-compass theorem with numerical coordinates and quadratic solutions for Pisan estate partitioning among consortes.

Vertex Partition (fol. 67v)

Boundary Point $D$ (fol. 68v)

Draw $EZ \parallel AD$ from midpoint $E$, cut along $DZ$

Parallel to Base (fol. 73v)

On $(10, 14, 12)$ for $\lambda = \frac{1}{2}$: $AE^2 = 50, AZ^2 = 72, EZ^2 = 98$

$c^2 = \frac{q a^2 + p b^2}{p + q}$; for $(7, 17)$, $c = 13$
