Classical, Medieval & Renaissance Sources¶

Elements (Books I–XIII, with Book XIV by Hypsicles) (euclid:elements)¶

  • Author / Editor: Euclid of Alexandria (fl. c. 300 BC) & Hypsicles (2nd century BC)

  • Edition / Locus: ed. J. L. Heiberg (Teubner, 1883–1888); trans. T. L. Heath (Cambridge, 1926); GEOMETOR Euclid Codex

On Divisions of Figures (Peri Diaireseōn Bibliōn) (euclid:divisions)¶

  • Author / Editor: Euclid of Alexandria (fl. c. 300 BC)

  • Edition / Locus: Restored from Arabic MS Paris BN 2457.14 and Fibonacci’s Practica Geometriae Dist. IV by R. C. Archibald (Cambridge University Press, 1915)

Measurement of a Circle (Dimensio Circuli) (archimedes:circle)¶

  • Author / Editor: Archimedes of Syracuse (c. 287 – 212 BC)

  • Edition / Locus: ed. J. L. Heiberg; Latin trans. Gerard of Cremona; GEOMETOR Archimedes Codex

On the Sphere and Cylinder, Books I–II (archimedes:sphere)¶

  • Author / Editor: Archimedes of Syracuse (c. 287 – 212 BC)

  • Edition / Locus: ed. J. L. Heiberg; transmitted via Banū Mūsā Verba filiorum

Metrica, Books I–III (heron:metrica)¶

  • Author / Editor: Heron of Alexandria (1st century AD)

  • Edition / Locus: ed. H. Schöne (Teubner, 1903); Book I.8 (area of triangle); Book III (division of figures)

Almagest, Book I, Ch. 10 (ptolemy:almagest)¶

  • Author / Editor: Claudius Ptolemy (c. 100 – c. 170 AD)

  • Edition / Locus: Latin trans. Gerard of Cremona (Toledo, 1175)

Verba filiorum Moysi filii Sekir (banu-musa:verba)¶

  • Author / Editor: Banū Mūsā (9th century; Latin trans. Gerard of Cremona)

  • Edition / Locus: ed. M. Clagett, Archimedes in the Middle Ages, Vol. I (1964), pp. 223–367

Liber embadorum (savasorda:embadorum)¶

  • Author / Editor: Abraham bar Ḥiyya / Savasorda (Latin trans. Plato of Tivoli, 1145)

  • Edition / Locus: ed. M. Curtze (Teubner, 1902), pp. 1–183

De Divina Proportione (pacioli:divina)¶

  • Author / Editor: Luca Pacioli, O.F.M. (1509)

  • Edition / Locus: Venice: Paganino Paganini, 1509; GEOMETOR Pacioli Codex
