NOTES — DISTINCTIO II: EXTRACTION OF SQUARE ROOTS¶

Philological Commentary, Classical Lineage, and Exact Symbolic Verification

English Edition · Latin (1220 / Boncompagni 1862) · Summary

Section 1: The Positional and Heronian Convergence Lineage¶

Leonardo’s square root method in Distinctio II represents a synthesis of three ancient traditions:

  1. Indian and Arabic Positional Arithmetic: Derived from al-Khwārizmī and Indian dust-board reckoning, utilizing digit pairing from the units place.

  2. Heronian Mean Iteration: Formulated in Hero of Alexandria’s Metrica I. 8 (heron:metrica):

    \[x_{n+1} = \frac{1}{2}\left(x_n + \frac{N}{x_n}\right).\]

  3. Duodecimal Pisan Scaling: Exact integer scaling into denarii (\(36 = 6^2\)) and square unciae (\(324 = 18^2\)), ensuring that irrational roots can be expressed to arbitrary precision in native surveyor’s subdivisions.

Exact verification in SymPy:

import sympy as sp

        def fibonacci_sqrt_step(n, a1):
            return a1 - (a1**2 - n) / (2 * a1)

        N = sp.Integer(10)
        a0 = sp.Integer(3)
        a1 = a0 + sp.Rational(1, 6)  # 19/6
        a2 = fibonacci_sqrt_step(N, a1)
        assert a2 == sp.Rational(721, 228)
        assert abs(float(a2) - float(sp.sqrt(10))) < 3e-5
