Concordance¶

Linking Leonardo Pisano’s Loci to Euclid, Heron, Archimedes, Pacioli, and the GEOMETOR Canon

This concordance maps the principal propositions and algebraic constructions of Leonardo Pisano’s Practica Geometriae (1220), Flos (1225), and Liber Abaci (1202/1228) across ten sister repositories in the GEOMETOR Digital Scriptorium and symbolic engine.

Master Concordance of Fibonacci Loci¶

Codex Section

Boncompagni (1857–1862) Locus

Mathematical & Constructive Core

GEOMETOR Sister Canon Links

Distinctio I

Prologus, Introductoria & Dist. I (Vol. II, pp. 1–18, fol. 1r–11v)

Euclidean definitions; Pisan pertica ($6$ pedes, $36$ denarii, $66$-pertica stariorum); Book II identities & III.35 chords

euclid (I.def.1–23, II.1–10, III.35) · elements (G.1–G.58) · pacioli (Summa Dist. IX) · wildberger (lattice quadrance)

Distinctio II

Distinctio II (Vol. II, pp. 18–30, fol. 11v–18v)

Positional, sexagesimal, and Pisan uncia square-root extraction via Euclid II.4 gnomon completion

euclid (II.4) · heron (Metrica I.8 root iteration) · archimedes (rational bounds for $\sqrt{3}$)

Distinctio III

Distinctio III, Pars I (Vol. II, pp. 39–41, fol. 23v–25r)

Scalene cathetus & casus on $(13, 14, 15)$; complete Euclidean incenter proof of Heron’s formula $\Delta = \sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}$

heron (Metrica I.8) · euclid (I.9, I.12, II.12–13, IV.4) · etc ($X(1)$ Incenter) · wildberger (Archimedes’ quadrea)

Distinctio III

Distinctio III, Pars IV (Vol. II, pp. 88–91, fol. 53v–55v)

Small-number 96-gon bisection ($1440 : 458\frac{1}{5}$ and $1440 : 458\frac{4}{9}$), yielding $\pi_{\text{Fib}} = \frac{864}{275}$ and $\frac{864}{275 - 1/11} = \frac{22}{7}$

archimedes (Dimensio Circuli Props. 1 & 3) · euclid (I.9, III.18, VI.3, XII.2)

Distinctio IV

Distinctio IV, Pars I (Vol. II, pp. 110–121, fol. 67v–74v)

Preservation of Euclid’s On Divisions of Figures: cuts from vertex, side point $D$, interior/exterior point, parallel to base (VI.13), & centroid $X(2)$

euclid (On Divisions, I.31, I.37–38, VI.2, VI.13, VI.19, VI.28–29) · etc ($X(2)$ Centroid) · pappus (Synagoge VII)

Distinctio IV

Distinctio IV, Pars II–IV (Vol. II, pp. 121–148, fol. 74v–92v)

Trapezoid parallel cut $c = \sqrt{(q a^2 + p b^2)/(p+q)}$ ($(7^2, 13^2, 17^2)$ progression), quadrilaterals, polygons & circular arcs

euclid (On Divisions Props. 19–29) · heron (Metrica III) · wildberger (congruum quadrance triples)

Distinctio V

Distinctio V (Vol. II, pp. 148–158, fol. 92v–100r)

Extraction of cube roots from integer binomials $(a+b)^3$ and fractional sexagesimal approximations

euclid (X) · heron (Metrica II) · al-khwarizmi

Distinctio VI

Distinctio VI (Vol. II, pp. 158–202, fol. 100v–130v)

Solid mensuration; all five regular polyhedra in sphere $d = 6$ via $\phi$ ($e_{12} = \sqrt{15} - \sqrt{3}$) & Hypsicles Book XIV

euclid (II.11, VI.30, XII.7–18, XIII.8–18, XIV) · archimedes (Sphere & Cylinder) · pacioli (De Divina Proportione) · divine ($\phi$ sections)

Distinctio VII

Distinctio VII (Vol. II, pp. 202–206, fol. 131r–133v)

Optical sighting of heights, valley depths & plains via rods and the 12-part quadrans shadow square

euclid (Optics Props. 18–21, VI.4) · pappus (projective sighting rays)

Distinctio VIII

Distinctio VIII (pp. 207–224) & Flos (pp. 227–247)

Pentagon & decagon in $d = 10$ via census census ($x^4 + 3125 = 125x^2$) & XIII.9–10; Flos cubic $1;22,7,42,33,4,40$

euclid (IV.10–11, X.36–73, XIII.9–10) · pacioli (algebraic problems in diameter 10)

Liber Abbaci I

Liber Abbaci Capitulum I (Vol. I, pp. 1–10, fol. 1r–4r)

The nine Indian figures $9\ 8\ 7\ 6\ 5\ 4\ 3\ 2\ 1$, the zero $0$ (zephirum), decimal positional notation, and finger reckoning

pacioli (Summa Dist. I) · elements (Positional arithmetic)

Liber Abbaci XII

Liber Abbaci Cap. XII, Pars 7 (Vol. I, pp. 283–284, fol. 124r)

Rabbit problem (Quot paria coniculorum), recurrence $F_{n+1} = F_n + F_{n-1}$, Cassini’s identity, and convergence to $\phi$

divine (convergents to $\phi$) · phyllotaxis (parastichy numbers $1..377$)
