Canonical Problem: Laying Out One Stariorum¶

  • Given: A plot of land whose area must equal exactly one Pisan stariorum (\(\Delta = 66\) superficial perticae), with a fixed frontage of \(w = 7\) perticae.

  • Construct: Divide \(66\) by \(7\):

    \[\text{Depth} = \frac{66}{7} = 9\frac{3}{7} \text{ perticae}.\]

  • Demonstrate: In Pisan subdivision:

    \[\frac{3}{7} \text{ pertica} = \frac{18}{7} \text{ pedes} = 2 \text{ pedes} + \frac{4}{7} \text{ pes} = 2 \text{ pedes} + \frac{72}{7} \text{ unciae} = 2\text{p } 10\frac{2}{7}\text{in}.\]

    Thus the exact dimensions are \(7 \text{ perticae} \times \left(9\text{p } 2\text{ft } 10\frac{2}{7}\text{in}\right) = 66 \text{ perticae}\).
