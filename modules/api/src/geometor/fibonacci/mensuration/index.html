src.geometor.fibonacci.mensuration¶

Exact symbolic Pisan metrology, Heron’s incenter proof, and 96-gon circle bounds (Practica Geometriae, Dist. I & III).

Grounded directly in Baldassarre Boncompagni’s 1862 critical Latin edition (Scritti di Leonardo Pisano, Vol. II: Leonardi Pisani Practica Geometriae ed Opuscoli, Rome, 1862):

  • Pisan Surveyor’s Metrology (Introductoria & Distinctio I, pp. 3–5, fol. 2v–3v): Exact rational conversions across the Pisan rod (pertica), pes, uncia, denarius, scala, panorum, stariorum ($66$ square perticae), and modiorum ($24$ stariora).

  • Heron’s Formula & Geometric Incenter Proof (Distinctio III, pp. 39–41, fol. 23v–25r): Exact Model straightedge-and-compass construction of incenter $T$, perpendiculars $TH, TE, TZ$, tangent excesses $(s-a, s-b, s-c)$, and semiperimeter extension on Fibonacci’s $(13, 14, 15)$ scalene triangle, alongside symbolic polynomial verification over $\mathbb{Z}[a, b, c]$.

  • Fibonacci’s Circle Measurement (Distinctio III, pp. 88–91, fol. 53v–55v): Fibonacci’s small-number Pisan reformulation of Archimedes’ 96-gon ($1440 : 458\frac{1}{5}$ and $1440 : 458\frac{4}{9}$), yielding $\pi_{\text{Fib}} = \frac{1440}{458\frac{1}{3}} = \frac{864}{275}$ and his exact rational identity $\frac{864}{275 - \frac{1}{11}} = \frac{22}{7}$.

src.geometor.fibonacci.mensuration.verify_heron_polynomial_identity() → sympy.Expr¶

Verify symbolically over $\mathbb{Z}[x, y, z]$ that $r^2 (x+y+z)^2 = (x+y+z) x y z$.

In Fibonacci’s geometric proof (Distinctio III, pp. 40–41), a triangle with tangent segments $x = s - a$, $y = s - b$, $z = s - c$ and inradius $r$ satisfies both $\Delta = r(x + y + z)$ and the similar-triangle proportion $r^2 (x + y + z) = x y z$ (since $\tan(\alpha/2)\tan(\beta/2) + \tan(\beta/2)\tan(\gamma/2) + \tan(\gamma/2)\tan(\alpha/2) = 1$). Returns 0 after exact algebraic simplification.

src.geometor.fibonacci.mensuration.construct_heron_incenter_proof() → HeronProofResult¶

Construct Fibonacci’s geometric proof of Heron’s formula (Distinctio III, pp. 40–41).

Uses the canonical $(13, 14, 15)$ triangle $A=(5, 12)$, $B=(0, 0)$, $G=(14, 0)$ so that every bisector, perpendicular foot, tangent segment, and auxiliary construction point has 100% exact rational coordinates in $\mathbb{Q}$:

  • Sides: $AB = 13$, $BG = 14$, $GA = 15$, semiperimeter $s = 21$.

  • Incenter: $T = (6, 4)$ ($X(1)$), inradius $r = TH = TE = TZ = 4$.

  • Tangent excesses: $AE = AZ = 7 = s - BG$, $BE = BH = 6 = s - GA$, $GH = GZ = 8 = s - AB$.

  • Extended base point: $L = (21, 0)$ on ray $BG$ with $GL = AE = 7$, so $BL = s = 21$ and $\operatorname{Area}(\triangle ABG) = TH \cdot BL = 4 \times 21 = 84 = \sqrt{21 \cdot 7 \cdot 6 \cdot 8}$.
