src.geometor.fibonacci.divisions¶

Exact Euclidean constructions for Practica Geometriae, Distinctio IV (De divisione camporum).

Preserves and formalizes in geometor.model Leonardo Pisano’s celebrated 1220 Latin transmission and expansion of Euclid’s lost treatise *On Divisions of Figures* (Boncompagni 1862, Vol. II, pp. 110–148):

  1. Dividing a Triangle from a Point on a Side (pp. 111–112, fol. 68v–69r): Straightedge-and-compass division of $\triangle ABG$ in exact ratio $p : q$ by a line $[DZ]$ drawn from any given point $D$ on side $[BG]$, using Euclid I.31 parallel transfer $[EZ] \parallel [AD]$ and Euclid I.37 equal-area shearing ($\triangle ADE = \triangle ADZ$).

  2. Dividing a Triangle Parallel to the Base (p. 119, fol. 73r–73v): Straightedge-and-compass division of $\triangle ABG$ in exact ratio $p : q$ by $[EZ] \parallel [BG]$ via Euclid VI.13 semicircle mean proportional $AE = \sqrt{\frac{p}{p+q}}\, AB$.

  3. Concurrency of Medians & Three-Consort Centroid Division (pp. 112–113, 120, fol. 69r–74r): Fibonacci’s Preparatoria proving the $2 : 1$ concurrency of medians at centroid $D$ ($X(2)$) and partitioning $\triangle ABG$ into three equal consort triangles $\triangle DAB, \triangle DBG, \triangle DGA$.

  4. Dividing a Trapezoid Parallel to its Bases (Distinctio IV, Pars II): Root-mean-square chord $c = \sqrt{\frac{q a^2 + p b^2}{p + q}}$, linking planar field division to three squares in arithmetic progression ($7^2, 13^2, 17^2$) in Liber Quadratorum (1225).

Divide $\triangle ABG$ $(13, 14, 15)$ in ratio $p : q$ from point $D$ on $[BG]$ (Boncompagni p. 112).

Assumes $D$ lies between $B$ and the fractional base division point $E$ (so $BD < BE$, i.e., $Z$ falls on side $[AG]$ just as in Fibonacci’s figure on p. 112 where $BD = 5$, $BE = 7$, $DG = 9$, and $GZ = 11\frac{2}{3}$).

src.geometor.fibonacci.divisions.construct_triangle_parallel_division(*, p: int = 1, q: int = 1, preset: str = '10-14-12') → ParallelDivisionResult¶

Divide $\triangle ABG$ in ratio $p : q$ by $[EZ] \parallel [BG]$ via Euclid VI.13 mean proportional.

Supports two exact symbolic presets:

  • "10-14-12" (default): Fibonacci’s exact numerical triangle on Boncompagni p. 119 ($AB = 10$, $BG = 14$, $GA = 12$), where $p = q = 1$ yields $AE^2 = 50$, $AZ^2 = 72$, $EZ^2 = 98$.

  • "13-14-15": Heronian triangle $A=(5, 12)$, $B=(0, 0)$, $G=(14, 0)$.

src.geometor.fibonacci.divisions.trapezoid_parallel_division(top_base_a: int | sympy.Expr = 7, bottom_base_b: int | sympy.Expr = 17, height: int | sympy.Expr = 10, *, p: int | sympy.Expr = 1, q: int | sympy.Expr = 1) → TrapezoidDivisionResult¶

Compute the exact parallel dividing line of a trapezoid (Distinctio IV, Pars II).

For parallel bases $a$ (top) and $b$ (bottom), the parallel chord $c$ cutting off upper area : lower area $= p : q$ satisfies $c^2 = \frac{q a^2 + p b^2}{p + q}$. On $(a, b) = (7, 17)$ with $p = q = 1$, $c = \sqrt{(49 + 289)/2} = 13$, exhibiting the arithmetic progression of squares $(7^2, 13^2, 17^2)$ with congruum $120$ celebrated in Fibonacci’s Liber Quadratorum (1225).
