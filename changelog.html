0.1.0 (2026-11-12)¶

Initial scholarly Digital Scriptorium 4-track Book Codex and exact symbolic SymPy / geometor.model construction suite for Leonardo Pisano (Fibonacci): Practica Geometriae (1220), with foundational bridges from Flos (1225) and Liber Abaci (1202/1228).

  • 4-Track Synchronized Book Codex (docsrc/practica_geometriae/):

    • Diplomatic public-domain Latin edition grounded in Baldassarre Boncompagni, Scritti di Leonardo Pisano, Vol. II (Leonardi Pisani Practica Geometriae ed Opuscoli, Rome, 1862) and Vol. I (Il Liber Abbaci, Rome, 1857).

    • Original GEOMETOR English translations, structural summaries, and symbolic commentary across 5 synchronized units (20 :layout: book RST files) covering the Prologue to Master Dominicus, Pisan agrarian metrology, all eight Distinctiones, Flos (1225), and Liber Abaci Cap. XII.

    • Runtime geometor.fibonacci.sphinx_ext registering the Latin (1220 / Boncompagni 1862) companion track and offline-safe local intersphinx bindings across sister GEOMETOR repositories.

  • Exact Symbolic Geometry Engine (src/geometor/fibonacci/):

    • mensuration.py: Pisan pertica / stariorum ($66$ perticae) algebra, Heron polynomial identity over $mathbb{Z}[x, y, z]$, Euclidean incenter proof on $(13, 14, 15)$ ($T = (6, 4)$, $r = 4$, $Delta = 84$), and Fibonacci’s 96-gon circle bounds ($frac{6480}{2063} < pi < frac{7200}{2291}$, mean $frac{864}{275}$).

    • divisions.py: Exact compass-and-straightedge reconstructions of Euclid’s lost On Divisions of Figures (Distinctio IV)—boundary-point cuts ($GZ = frac{35}{3}$), semicircle mean-proportional parallel cuts on $(10, 14, 12)$ ($AE^2 = 50, AZ^2 = 72, EZ^2 = 98$), median concurrency at centroid $X(2)$, and Babylonian/Euclidean trapezoid root-mean-square chords.

    • polyhedra_golden.py: Exact radical metrics and Euclid XIII.18 section for all five Platonic solids in sphere diameter $d = 6$, Hypsicles Book XIV volume ratio $sqrt{frac{5 + sqrt{5}}{6}}$, golden chord division ($sqrt{15} - sqrt{3}$), Distinctio VIII pentagon/decagon in $d = 10$ via census census quartic $x^4 + 3125 = 125x^2$, Quadrans optical surveying, Flos sexagesimal cubic ($1; 22, 7, 42, 33, 4, 40$) and congruum of $5$, and Liber Abaci rabbit convergents to $phi$.

    • render.py & scripts/build_figures.py: 10 theme-adaptive interactive .euclid-graphic SVGs with 3-vertex midpoint paths and bidirectional :g: / :eq: highlighting.

  • Scholarly Apparatus & Demos (resources/, demos/, tests/):

    • Complete JSON registries (sources_manifest.json, concordance.json, persons.json, citations.json, glossary.json) powering hover cards and cross-canon concordance tables.

    • Runnable Rich CLI demonstrations and full pytest verification suite.
