Leonardo Pisano (Fibonacci)
(c. 1170 – c. 1245 · Citizen & Mathematician of Pisa)

First Composition: Pisa (1202)
Enlarged Revision: Dedicated to Master Michael Scot (1228)
Critical Edition: Baldassarre Boncompagni (Il Liber Abbaci, Roma, 1857)
